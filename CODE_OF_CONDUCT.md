# Community Guidelines

TwoPoint Development welcomes respectful, constructive participation.

- Be respectful and focused on the project.
- Provide reproducible technical details.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or impersonate others.
- Do not publish private information, credentials, or server secrets.
- Do not use project spaces to advertise unrelated paid services or resources.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that does not follow these guidelines and may restrict participation when necessary.
